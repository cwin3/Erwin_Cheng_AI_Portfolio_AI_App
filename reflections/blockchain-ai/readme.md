# 🔗 Reflection: AI and Blockchain

## 💭 What I Learned
AI and blockchain complement each other. AI analyzes data, while blockchain ensures that the data is secure and trustworthy.

---

## 🔍 What Surprised Me
I didn’t expect blockchain to play such an important role in improving AI reliability.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges I Noticed
- Scalability issues  
- Energy consumption  
- Difficulty integrating different systems  

---

## 🔗 Real-World Connection
AI and blockchain are used in supply chains, healthcare, and data security systems.

---

## 🧠 How My Thinking Changed
I now see that combining technologies can solve problems that one technology alone cannot.
