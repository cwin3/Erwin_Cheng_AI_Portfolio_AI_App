# 🏭 Case Study: AI in Manufacturing

## 📌 Problem
Manufacturing processes often face inefficiencies, defects, and unexpected equipment failures.

---

## ⚙️ AI Solution
AI systems use sensors, machine learning, and computer vision to monitor production and optimize processes.

---

## 📊 Results
- Reduced downtime  
- Improved product quality  
- Increased efficiency  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges
- High setup cost  
- Integration with existing systems  
- Workforce training  

---

## 💭 Reflection
I learned that AI is not just about automation—it’s about improving consistency and reducing waste.

---

## 🧠 Key Learning
AI works best when integrated across the entire production system, not just one part.
