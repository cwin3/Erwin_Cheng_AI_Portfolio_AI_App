# 💳 Reflection: AI in Finance

## 💭 What I Learned
I learned that AI has become a core part of modern finance, not just an add-on. It improves how banks detect fraud, manage risk, and make decisions faster using real-time data.

AI allows systems to analyze huge amounts of financial data instantly, which helps detect suspicious activity before damage is done.

---

## 🔍 What Surprised Me
What surprised me most is how AI can monitor transactions in real time instead of relying on traditional methods that detect fraud after it happens.

I also didn’t expect how much personalization is involved, especially in things like loan approvals and customer services.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges I Noticed
- High dependence on data quality  
- False positives affecting customer experience  
- Need for continuous updates and monitoring  
- Privacy and regulatory concerns  

---

## 🔗 Real-World Connection
Banks already use AI for:
- Fraud detection  
- Risk analysis  
- Loan approvals  
- Algorithmic trading  

These systems are now essential for staying competitive.

---

## 🧠 How My Thinking Changed
Before this, I thought AI in finance was mostly about automation. Now I understand it’s more about improving decision-making, accuracy, and speed.

---

## 💡 Key Insight
AI is not just replacing tasks—it is transforming how financial systems operate at a fundamental level.
