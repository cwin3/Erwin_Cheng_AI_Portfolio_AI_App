# ⚖️ Case Study: Ethical and Societal Implications of Autonomous Vehicles

## 📌 Overview
This case study explores how artificial intelligence is used in autonomous vehicles (self-driving cars) and the ethical and societal challenges that come with it.

Autonomous vehicles aim to reduce human error, improve safety, and make transportation more efficient. However, they also introduce complex questions about responsibility, fairness, and trust.

---

## ⚙️ How the AI System Works
Autonomous vehicles rely on a combination of technologies:

- Cameras, sensors, and radar to collect environmental data  
- Machine learning algorithms to interpret surroundings  
- Real-time decision-making systems to control movement  

### Basic Process:
1. Collect data from environment  
2. Analyze and interpret surroundings  
3. Make driving decisions  
4. Execute actions (steering, braking, acceleration)  

---

## 📊 Benefits
- Reduced accidents caused by human error  
- Improved traffic efficiency  
- Increased accessibility for elderly and disabled individuals  
- Enhanced convenience for users  

---

## ⚠️ Ethical Challenges

### 1. Decision-Making in Critical Situations
In unavoidable accidents, how should the AI decide what action to take?

### 2. Accountability
If an accident occurs:
- Who is responsible?  
  - Developer  
  - Manufacturer  
  - Owner  

### 3. Bias in AI Systems
AI models depend on data. If the data is biased, the system may not perform equally well for all people.

### 4. Transparency
Many AI systems operate as “black boxes,” making decisions difficult to explain.

### 5. Privacy Concerns
Autonomous vehicles collect large amounts of user and location data.

---

## 🌍 Societal Impact

- Job displacement (drivers, delivery workers)  
- Increased inequality due to high costs  
- Challenges in building public trust  

---

## ⚠️ Risks & Limitations
- Poor performance in complex environments (weather, construction zones)  
- Cybersecurity threats (hacking risks)  
- Over-reliance on automation  

---

## 💭 Reflection
This assignment helped me realize that AI is not just about improving technology—it also involves making difficult ethical decisions.

Autonomous vehicles show that even when AI has clear benefits, it still raises questions that don’t have simple answers. It made me think more critically about how technology should be designed and used responsibly.

---

## 🧠 Key Learning
AI systems must balance performance with ethics, safety, and transparency to be truly effective in real-world applications.
