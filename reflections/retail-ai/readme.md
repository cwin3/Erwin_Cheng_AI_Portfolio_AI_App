# 🛒 Reflection: AI in Retail

## 💭 What I Learned
AI improves the shopping experience by personalizing recommendations and optimizing inventory management.

---

## 🔍 What Surprised Me
I didn’t realize how much AI works behind the scenes in retail, especially in supply chains and demand prediction.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges I Noticed
- Data privacy concerns  
- Integration with existing systems  
- Difficulty measuring ROI  

---

## 🔗 Real-World Connection
Online stores and physical retailers use AI for recommendations, chatbots, and automated checkout systems.

---

## 🧠 How My Thinking Changed
I now understand that AI in retail is not just about customer experience—it also improves operations behind the scenes.
