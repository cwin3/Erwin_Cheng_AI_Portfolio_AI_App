# 🧠 Key Learnings

## 1. AI is Data-Driven
The performance of AI systems depends heavily on the quality of data used.

---

## 2. Real-World AI is Complex
Implementing AI is much more challenging than building models.

---

## 3. Ethics is Essential
Bias, privacy, and transparency must be considered in every AI system.

---

## 4. Human + AI Collaboration
AI works best as a support tool rather than a replacement for humans.
