# 🤖 Case Study: AGI in Manufacturing

## 📌 Problem
Traditional AI systems are limited to specific tasks and lack adaptability.

---

## ⚙️ AI Solution
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) aims to create systems that can adapt across multiple tasks and make complex decisions.

---

## 📊 Results (Expected)
- Fully optimized production systems  
- Adaptive decision-making  
- Increased innovation  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges
- High cost and complexity  
- Ethical concerns  
- Workforce impact  

---

## 💭 Reflection
This topic helped me understand the future potential of AI and how it could move beyond narrow applications.

---

## 🧠 Key Learning
AGI represents a major shift from task-based automation to intelligent system management.
