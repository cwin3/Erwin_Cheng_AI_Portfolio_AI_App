# 🏛️ Case Study: AI in Government Systems

## 📌 Problem
Traditional government systems are often slow, reactive, and inefficient.

---

## ⚙️ AI Solution
AI systems analyze large datasets to improve infrastructure planning, traffic management, and public services.

---

## 📊 Results
- Improved efficiency in public services  
- Predictive infrastructure maintenance  
- Better decision-making  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges
- Privacy concerns  
- High implementation costs  
- Data bias risks  

---

## 💭 Reflection
This case study showed how AI can improve large-scale systems, but also how difficult it is to balance innovation with responsibility.

---

## 🧠 Key Learning
AI can transform public systems, but ethical and privacy considerations must be addressed.
