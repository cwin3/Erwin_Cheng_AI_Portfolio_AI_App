# 🏥 Reflection: AI in Healthcare & Agriculture

## 💭 What I Learned
AI is transforming healthcare by helping doctors detect diseases earlier and make more personalized decisions. In agriculture, it helps farmers use resources more efficiently.

---

## 🔍 What Surprised Me
I didn’t expect how much AI relies on detailed data like genetics in healthcare or soil conditions in farming.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges I Noticed
- Data privacy concerns in healthcare  
- High cost of AI systems  
- Accessibility issues for smaller farms  

---

## 🔗 Real-World Connection
AI is already being used for medical imaging, robotic surgery, and precision farming.

---

## 🧠 How My Thinking Changed
I now see AI as a tool that enhances decision-making rather than replacing professionals.
